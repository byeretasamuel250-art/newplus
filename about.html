What is newplus.app?

newplus.app is a free app for finding and posting jobs and gigs near you in Uganda. Anyone can post a job or gig/task, casual work, whatever needs doing and anyone nearby looking for work can browse gigs, find one that fits, and message the poster directly to sort out the details.

How it works

1. Create a profile. Sign up with your phone number, pick a 4-digit PIN, and add your name, date of birth, and a profile photo.

2. Post a gig, or browse gigs nearby. Posting is as simple as sharing a status. say what the work is, and it's visible to people nearby right away. Looking for work? Browse what's posted near you, sorted by district or distance.

3. Message the poster. Found a gig that fits (or someone interested in the one you posted)? Message them directly, right from your App, to work out the details.

Privacy

Sharing your location is optional. if you skip it, you're just grouped by district instead, and no one ever sees your exact address. Your conversations about a gig are private between you and the other person.

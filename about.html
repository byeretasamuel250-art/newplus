What is newplus.app?

newplus.app is a free website for finding houses for rent in Uganda. Every listing shows photos, the monthly rent, and where the house is, so you can see what is available before you take the next step. No sign-up and no app download needed.

How it works

1. Browse houses. Open newplus.app and scroll through available houses, grouped by district. Tap any house to see all of its photos and details.

2. Search and filter. Looking for a particular place? Search by area or house type, or tap a district to see only the houses there.

3. Chat with us. Found a house you like? Tap Chat Us and WhatsApp opens with a ready message about that house, so you can confirm it is still available and arrange a viewing.

Always up to date

New houses appear on the site as soon as they are added, and houses that are taken are removed, so what you see is what is currently available.

Who runs newplus?

newplus is operated by BING-TECH, based in Kampala, Uganda. Our goal is simple: make finding a rental home easier, clearer, and faster for everyone in Uganda.
